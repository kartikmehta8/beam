# Asset origins

- Beam glyph, ENS and World ID marks come from the Beam Android app brand sources and partner icon paths. The blue favicon combines the app glyph and the app icon’s #0080BC background.
- Inter and Roboto Mono webfonts come from the Orb site. Their license files accompany them. Tabler Icons are generated as a local sprite and retain the included MIT license.
- The guided preview is rendered from HTML, CSS and fixture data. The phone uses React Bits Tilted Card. Count Up animates the sample draft amount, and Spotlight Card follows the pointer in the guide and agent demo. See its provenance and license under `src/react-bits/`.
